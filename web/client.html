Tips for iPhone
  • Photo Library may convert HEIC photos to JPEG. To keep originals, save them to Files first (Share → Save to Files), then use Choose File.
  • If "Optimize iPhone Storage" is on, some photos live in iCloud only and can't be sent offline.
  • Don't lock the screen or switch apps while sending.
